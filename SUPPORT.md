# Support

- **Questions and how-to**: read the [documentation site](https://protocolith.github.io/protocolith/docs/) first
  ([FAQ](docs/FAQ.md), [error codes](docs/reference/error-codes.md)). Every error line from the CLI ends with a hint.
- **Bugs and feature requests**: open an issue with the templates at
  <https://github.com/protocolith/protocolith/issues/new/choose>. Include the output of `protocolith --version`,
  `protocolith summary` and the failing command; never paste secrets or raw data.
- **Security problems**: do not open a public issue; follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- **Conduct**: see [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
- **Contributing**: see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

Protocolith is maintained by one person without a service-level promise; replies may take days.
